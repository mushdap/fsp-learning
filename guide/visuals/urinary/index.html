    Harnleiter. Harnröhre. Your tongue is allowed a practice run.

    These names are easy to mix up. The ureters carry urine from the kidneys to the bladder. The urethra carries it from the bladder to the outside. Trace the route with a finger first. Your brain appreciates a map; it has filed a formal complaint about memorising similar nouns without one.

    Now explain it without the drawing¶

    „Der Harnleiter verbindet die Niere mit der Blase. Die Harnröhre führt von der Blase nach außen.“

    If you switch the words, correct yourself calmly. In real conversations, noticing and repairing an error is a strength. The map shows general anatomy; a person's symptoms still need an individual assessment.
