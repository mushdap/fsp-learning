    PDF coverage audit¶

    The guide does not yet cover every detail in the PDF. All 255 physical pages are accounted for below. A page being audited is not the same as its material being taught or assessed.

    The audit identifies 124 learning-objective groups: before the new supplements, 27 were covered at the stated narrow scope, 75 partly covered and 22 missing. Original bilingual learning notes and review questions have now been added below. These notes do not close detailed clinical, practical-examination or assessment gaps by themselves.

    The glossary inventory contains 336 source spellings, including variants and repeated concepts. Only 29 have an exact matching German word-card term in the current cases. This is a reproducible string-match baseline, not a semantic coverage percentage. Open the terminology checklist.

    Important source distinctions¶

    • PDF page 234 describes MRI contrast as iodine-based. Standard MRI contrast commonly uses gadolinium; MRI screening and preparation depend on the examination. ACR/RSNA MRI safety.
    • The glossary repeatedly equates appendicitis with a perforation. Appendicitis is inflammation of the appendix; perforation is a possible complication. NHS appendicitis.
    • PDF page 251 confuses visual acuity and confrontation visual-field testing. These are distinct examination components. Merck examination reference.
    • Blanket promises of no complications, universal fasting times, automatic medicine interruption and historical exam predictions are not transferred as instructions. Procedure-specific decisions need current clinical guidance.

    Learning objectives and supplements¶

    Status describes the evidence before these supplementary notes. Clinical-detail rows remain incomplete teaching pathways. German learning notes here were authored directly; they are not generated by the page translation catalogue.

    Three tasks, three registers¶

    PDF physical pages: 7, 10, 11. Before audit: partial · Scope: communication.

    Register choice and honest documentation are practised, but the complete introduction, written report and examiner follow-up structure is not taught in these cases.

    Das Buch unterscheidet Patientengespräch, schriftliche Dokumentation und ärztliche Übergabe. Beim Patienten erklären Sie verständlich; im Bericht und im Kollegengespräch verwenden Sie passende Fachbegriffe. Eine kurze berufliche Vorstellung ist eine andere Aufgabe als die Vorstellung eines Patienten. Üben Sie auch Rückfragen, statt nur einen fertigen Vortrag aufzusagen.

    The book separates the patient conversation, written documentation and colleague handover. Use understandable language with the patient and suitable technical terms in the report and handover. A short professional introduction is different from presenting a patient. Practise follow-up questions as well as a prepared presentation.

    Lernfrage: Wie erklären Sie dieselbe Beschwerde einem Patienten und einem Kollegen?

    Review question: How would you explain the same symptom to a patient and a colleague?

    Related practice: foundations-dictionary-on-holiday, foundations-blank-box.

    History includes more than one box¶

    PDF physical pages: 8, 9. Before audit: partial · Scope: communication.

    Selected history skills exist; vaccination, menstrual history, height, previous treatment and systematic accompanying/vegetative symptoms are not comprehensively rehearsed.

    Eine strukturierte Anamnese umfasst aktuelle und begleitende Beschwerden, vegetative Funktionen sowie relevante Vorerkrankungen, Eingriffe und Behandlungen. Hinzu kommen Medikamente, Allergien, Impfungen und passende soziale und familiäre Angaben. Menstruationsfragen werden bei entsprechender Relevanz verständlich und respektvoll gestellt; ein Alter allein ersetzt keine individuelle Frage. Nicht erhobene Angaben bleiben als offen markiert.

    A structured history covers current and accompanying symptoms, vegetative functions, and relevant previous illnesses, procedures and treatments. Medicines, allergies, vaccination and relevant social and family information also belong in the framework. Ask relevant menstrual questions clearly and respectfully rather than substituting an age assumption for an individual answer. Mark information not obtained as outstanding.

    Lernfrage: Welche Anamnesebereiche fehlen noch, wenn nur Schmerz und Medikamente besprochen wurden?

    Review question: Which history areas remain if only pain and medicines have been discussed?

    Related practice: history-weight-clock, history-never-operated, history-home-support, history-family-maybe.

    Name, use and reaction¶

    PDF physical pages: 8, 9, 12. Before audit: covered · Scope: communication.

    Existing cases substantively teach verification, actual use, nonprescription products and reaction detail.

    Ein Medikamentenname ohne tatsächliche Anwendung ist unvollständig. Fragen Sie auch nach frei gekauften Mitteln und Darreichungsformen. Bei einer Allergie gehören Auslöser und erinnerte Reaktion in den Bericht; eine unklare Erinnerung wird nicht zur gesicherten Diagnose.

    A medicine name without actual use is incomplete. Ask about nonprescription products and formulations too. An allergy account includes the trigger and remembered reaction; uncertain memory must not become a confirmed diagnosis.

    Lernfrage: Welche Angaben fehlen bei „eine blaue Tablette und irgendeine Allergie“?

    Review question: What is missing from 'a blue tablet and some allergy'?

    Related practice: history-blue-tablet, history-herbal-counts, history-allergy-story.

    Reporting without inventing findings¶

    PDF physical pages: 10, 11, 19. Before audit: partial · Scope: communication.

    Examples demonstrate attribution and timing; no explicit systematic drill on reported speech, all report sections or handling examiner requests for exact details.

    In der Übergabe trennen Sie Angaben des Patienten, eigene Beobachtungen und noch fehlende Befunde. Formulierungen wie „sie berichtet“ oder passende indirekte Rede zeigen die Herkunft. Nennen Sie Beginn und spätere Verschlechterung getrennt. Eine verneinte Beschwerde ist etwas anderes als eine nicht gestellte Frage.

    Separate patient reports, your observations and missing findings in handover. Attribution such as 'she reports' or suitable reported speech identifies the source. Separate initial onset from later deterioration. A denied symptom is different from a question not asked.

    Lernfrage: Wie dokumentieren Sie eine nicht gestellte Fieberfrage?

    Review question: How would you document a fever question that was not asked?

    Related practice: foundations-three-days-three-weeks, foundations-blank-box, clots-negative-answer.

    Leg pain: pattern before label¶

    PDF physical pages: 11, 13, 14, 18. Before audit: covered · Scope: communication.

    Walking distance, rest symptoms, triggers, change and context are explicitly rehearsed.

    Erfragen Sie Seite, Ort, Beginn, Schmerzcharakter, Ausstrahlung und Verlauf. Bei belastungsabhängigen Beschwerden helfen Gehstrecke und Wirkung einer Pause. Eine neue Ruhekomponente gehört ausdrücklich in die Übergabe. Die Geschichte liefert Hinweise, noch keinen gesicherten Gefäßbefund.

    Ask about side, location, onset, character, radiation and progression. Walking distance and the effect of a pause help describe exertional symptoms. Explicitly hand over newly occurring rest symptoms. The history provides clues, not a confirmed vascular finding.

    Lernfrage: Was verändert sich an der Zusammenfassung, wenn Schmerzen nun auch in Ruhe auftreten?

    Review question: How does the summary change when pain now also occurs at rest?

    Related practice: circulation-bakery-bench, circulation-rest-is-different, circulation-pain-score-context.

    Peripheral arterial disease: assessment and classification¶

    PDF physical pages: 12, 15, 16, 17, 18, 21. Before audit: partial · Scope: clinical-detail.

    ABI, Fontaine staging, pulse sites, examination findings, differential diagnosis and revascularisation/treatment selection are not substantively taught. Source staging is internally problematic when rest pain is reported; its drug/stage recommendations require current clinical review.

    Das Buch behandelt körperliche Gefäßuntersuchung, Knöchel-Arm-Index, Duplexsonografie und die Fontaine-Einteilung. Diese Bausteine dienen unterschiedlichen Fragen: Symptome beschreiben, Durchblutung beurteilen und Befunde einordnen. Die bisherigen Geschichten erklären nur einen Teil davon. Stadienzuordnung und Therapieentscheidung bleiben eine gesonderte, fachlich zu prüfende Lernaufgabe.

    The book covers vascular examination, ankle–brachial index, duplex ultrasound and Fontaine classification. These answer different questions: describing symptoms, assessing circulation and interpreting findings. Existing stories explain only part of this material. Staging and treatment selection remain separate learning tasks needing clinical review.

    Lernfrage: Welche Teile der pAVK-Abklärung erklärt eine Geschichte über Gehstrecke noch nicht?

    Review question: Which parts of arterial disease assessment are not explained by a walking-distance story?

    Related practice: circulation-ultrasound-question, circulation-artery-vein.

    Who helps at home?¶
