    Peter Hahn · 67

    Start handover with Peter's current problem

    Peter has ongoing chest pressure and sweating. The clinical team has been called. Your handover starts with his job and old surgery. Change the order so the team hears the urgent problem first.

    Peter hat anhaltenden Brustdruck und Schweißausbruch. Das ärztliche Team ist gerufen. Ihre Übergabe beginnt mit Beruf und alter Operation. Ändern Sie die Reihenfolge: zuerst das aktuelle dringende Problem.

    Three clear stepsDrei klare Schritte

    1. 1First: ongoing chest pressureZuerst: anhaltender Brustdruck
    2. 2Then: timing and associated symptomsDann: Verlauf und Begleitbeschwerden
    3. 3State: known findings and missing informationAngeben: bekannte Befunde und fehlende Informationen

    German words · human meaning

    Try these words

    Listen to the patient. Ask one clear question at a time. Dr Daniyal is a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Haben Sie gesagt, dass der Druck jetzt noch da ist?

    English meaning

    Did you say that the pressure is still there now?

    Dr Daniyal

    Ja. Damit beginne ich die Übergabe.

    English meaning

    Yes. I will begin the handover with that.

    Patient

    Die anderen Sachen können später kommen?

    English meaning

    Can the other things come later?

    Dr Daniyal

    Zuerst kommen die aktuellen Beschwerden und die Informationen, die das Team jetzt braucht.

    English meaning

    The current symptoms and information the team needs now come first.

    Read the explanation

    Best next move: His ongoing chest pressure and urgent concernSein anhaltender Brustdruck und die dringende Sorge

    • His occupation and relevant previous operationSein Beruf und die relevante frühere Operation

      Background does not communicate the immediate priority first.Die Vorgeschichte nennt die unmittelbare Priorität nicht zuerst.

    • His full background before the present symptomsSeine ganze Vorgeschichte vor den aktuellen Beschwerden

      The urgent current problem should not be buried.Das dringende aktuelle Problem sollte nicht untergehen.

    • His ongoing chest pressure and urgent concernSein anhaltender Brustdruck und die dringende Sorge

      This immediately communicates why the team is needed.Das erklärt sofort, warum das Team benötigt wird.

    Where this exercise comes from¶

    Fictional practice case. The cited physical PDF pages inspire acute chest symptoms, urgency, investigation explanations and handover. This original fictional exercise focuses on lead an urgent handover with the immediate problem and explicit unknowns. The patient, setting, dialogue and decision are invented; no clinical outcome or exam prediction is claimed.

    Topic reference: FSP in Bayern 2024, Shamil Gurbanov, second edition, supplied PDF physical page(s) 40, 42. Physical page numbers count from the first PDF page; printed numbers are usually three lower. Dialogue, names and teaching choices are original adaptations.
