    The missing tick in the diary

    Beate uses a diary to mark each medicine dose but forgot yesterday’s tick. Now she cannot remember whether she forgot the tablet or only the pen. She asks whether taking extra today will solve the uncertainty. The diary is usually reliable, which makes the missing mark particularly irritating. You need to identify the medicine, timing and actual uncertainty, then obtain the appropriate prescriber or pharmacist advice. Do not invent a catch-up dose, and do not scold her into claiming certainty she does not have.

    Meet your patient
    Beate Holm, 70
    Practise

    Pull up a chair. Listen first.

    Beate uses a diary to mark each medicine dose but forgot yesterday’s tick. Dr Palwasha appears as a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Soll ich heute einfach mehr nehmen?

    English meaning

    Should I simply take more today?

    Dr Palwasha

    Bitte ändern Sie die Menge nicht auf eigene Faust. Welches Medikament betrifft es genau?

    English meaning

    Please do not change the amount on your own. Which medicine exactly is involved?

    Patient

    Ich weiß nicht, ob nur der Haken fehlt.

    English meaning

    I do not know whether only the tick is missing.

    Dr Palwasha

    Diese Unsicherheit geben wir bei der Rückfrage an Arzt oder Apotheke ausdrücklich an.

    English meaning

    We will explicitly mention that uncertainty when asking the doctor or pharmacy.

    Practice misstep

    The conversation gets stuck

    Practice misstep: Always double the next dose after a missing tick.

    Better approach

    Make room for understanding

    Clarify the exact medicine and what is known about the last dose, then seek medicine-specific professional advice. Preserve the uncertainty and avoid supplying a generic catch-up instruction.

    What changed—and why it helps¶

    A missing record and a missed medicine are not necessarily the same event. Accurate wording helps the adviser respond to the real uncertainty rather than an invented definite omission.
