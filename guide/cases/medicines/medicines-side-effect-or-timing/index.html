    After the tablet is not the same as because of it

    Rita reports nausea after starting a new medicine and is convinced the tablet caused it. She may be right, but the timing, other changes and severity have not yet been explored. She has arranged the packages on the desk in the order she suspects them, like a very small detective lineup. You need to take the concern seriously, describe the temporal association accurately and arrange clinical review. Do not confirm causation from timing alone or tell her to stop every medicine without an individual plan.

    Meet your patient
    Rita Schenk, 61
    Practise
    Distinguish temporal association from confirmed causation

    German words · human meaning

    Pull up a chair. Listen first.

    Rita reports nausea after starting a new medicine and is convinced the tablet caused it. Dr Palwasha appears as a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Seit der neuen Tablette ist mir übel.

    English meaning

    I have felt nauseated since starting the new tablet.

    Dr Palwasha

    Wann begann die Übelkeit genau, und welches Präparat wurde neu begonnen?

    English meaning

    Exactly when did the nausea begin, and which medicine was newly started?

    Patient

    Das möchte ich mit Ihnen anhand der Packung prüfen.

    English meaning

    I would like to check that with you using the package.

    Dr Palwasha

    Wir dokumentieren den zeitlichen Zusammenhang und lassen die Beschwerden beurteilen.

    English meaning

    We will record the timing and have the symptoms assessed.

    Practice misstep

    The conversation gets stuck

    Practice misstep: Anything starting after a tablet is definitely caused by it.

    Better approach

    Make room for understanding

    Clarify the medicine, timing, symptom severity and other relevant changes, then seek clinical review. Describe a possible association without declaring causation or improvising a blanket stop instruction.

    What changed—and why it helps¶

    Timing is an important clue, but it is not the whole explanation. Accurate wording respects the patient’s observation while leaving room for assessment of other possibilities.
