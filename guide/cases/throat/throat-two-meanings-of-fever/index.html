    Feeling hot without a measurement

    Jonas has a sore throat and says he has a high fever. He felt hot while carrying shopping. He has not measured his temperature. The referral already describes his fever as confirmed.

    Jonas hat Halsschmerzen. Er sagt, er habe hohes Fieber. Beim Tragen der Einkäufe war ihm heiß. Er hat nicht gemessen. Auf der Überweisung steht schon, dass Fieber bestätigt sei.

    See the differenceDen Unterschied erkennen

    1. 1Reported: feels hotBerichtet: fühlt sich heiß
    2. 2Measured temperature: unknownGemessene Temperatur: unbekannt
    3. 3Record the differenceDen Unterschied dokumentieren
    RememberMerken

    A feeling is not a measured temperature.

    Ein Gefühl ist kein Messwert.

    German words · human meaning

    Try these words

    Listen to the patient. Ask one clear question at a time. Dr Daniyal is a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Ich habe bestimmt hohes Fieber.

    English meaning

    I must have a high fever.

    Dr Daniyal

    Haben Sie Ihre Temperatur gemessen oder fühlen Sie sich heiß?

    English meaning

    Have you measured your temperature, or do you feel hot?

    Patient

    Gemessen habe ich nicht.

    English meaning

    I have not measured it.

    Dr Daniyal

    Dann notiere ich zunächst ein Fiebergefühl, ohne gemessenen Wert.

    English meaning

    Then I will initially record feeling feverish, without a measured value.

    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Record a temperature of 39 degrees without a measurement.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Ask whether and when Jonas measured his temperature. Record that he feels feverish, but no measured value is available. Arrange assessment.

    Why this helps¶

    Feeling hot is useful information. It does not tell you the exact temperature, so the record must show which information is missing.
