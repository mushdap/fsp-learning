    English meaning

    I will stay with you while help is coming.

    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Finish the family history before asking for help.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Stop the routine questions. Call the clinical team immediately. Stay with Emir while help comes and explain the action briefly. Do not call the problem harmless.

    Why this helps¶

    New breathing difficulty changes the priority. Calling the team must come before completing the history or giving a long explanation.

    Say it to a colleague

    Unterbrechen Sie die Routinefragen. Rufen Sie sofort das Behandlungsteam. Bleiben Sie bei Emir und erklären Sie kurz, was passiert. Verharmlosen Sie die Beschwerden nicht.
