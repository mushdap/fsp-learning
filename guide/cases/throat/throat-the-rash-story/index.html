    Paul's record lists penicillin allergy. He recalls red spots after an unnamed medicine years ago; other details are unclear. What can you safely record about Paul’s uncertain reaction?

    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Delete the allergy because Paul only remembers a rash.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Ask about the medicine, timing and other symptoms. Record what Paul remembers and what remains unclear. Keep the warning and seek earlier records where possible.

    Why this helps¶

    A vague memory does not prove a severe allergy. It also does not justify deleting a warning without further review.

    Patientenangabe · Gesprächszeile 2Patient account · Dialogue line 2

    So lässt es sich fachlich ausdrückenA professional way to express this

    Rote Flecken werden erinnert, der Name des damaligen Präparats jedoch nicht.Red spots are recalled, but the name of the product at the time is not.
