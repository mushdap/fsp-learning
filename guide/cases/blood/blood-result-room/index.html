    A serious possibility deserves a chair

    Sina has been asked to stay for a discussion after an initial assessment of fatigue and bruising. She hears the word leukaemia in a corridor conversation and assumes the whole team knows a diagnosis nobody will tell her. No confirmed result is supplied in this exercise. She is trying to remain composed while deciding whether to call her partner. Your task is to move the discussion into a private setting, establish what she heard and ensure the responsible clinician explains the actual evidence and uncertainty without speculation.

    Meet your patient
    Sina Beck, 35
    Practise
    Communicate concern without a corridor announcement
    Scroll to begin ↓
    German words · human meaning

    Pull up a chair. Listen first.

    Sina has been asked to stay for a discussion after an initial assessment of fatigue and bruising. Dr Palwasha appears as a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Ich habe auf dem Flur Leukämie gehört. Ist das meine Diagnose?

    English meaning

    I heard leukaemia in the corridor. Is that my diagnosis?

    Dr Palwasha

    Lassen Sie uns das in Ruhe und ungestört besprechen.

    English meaning

    Let us discuss that calmly and privately.

    Patient

    Soll ich meinen Partner anrufen?

    English meaning

    Should I call my partner?

    Dr Palwasha

    Wenn Sie Unterstützung möchten, organisieren wir das; die gesicherten Informationen erklärt das zuständige Team.

    English meaning

    If you would like support, we can arrange that; the responsible team will explain the confirmed information.

    Practice misstep

    The conversation gets stuck

    Practice misstep: Confirm the overheard word as her diagnosis.

    Better approach

    Make room for understanding

    Provide privacy, ask what Sina heard and acknowledge the distress caused by uncertainty. Verify what information is actually available and involve the responsible clinician in explaining it. Ask whether she wants a support person. Do not confirm a diagnosis from overheard language or deny every concern simply to undo the discomfort of the corridor encounter.

    What changed—and why it helps¶

    A serious possibility can become a perceived diagnosis when heard without context. Repair begins with a proper conversation, not another hurried sentence in public. The person needs accurate information at the right level and control over support. A calm setting helps, but it cannot replace checking the facts before speaking.

    A small decision

    Your turn

    Sina overheard leukaemia in a corridor. How should you arrange the next conversation?

    Read the explanation

    Best next move: Arrange a private evidence-based discussion with her chosen support

    The useful move repairs both setting and information. Overheard terminology is not enough to establish a diagnosis. An unverified denial may also be misleading. Check the actual findings, involve the clinician responsible for them and give Sina a choice about support while explaining what is known and what remains uncertain.
