    Nein. Die dringende Behandlung und Beurteilung haben jetzt Vorrang.

    English meaning

    No. Urgent treatment and assessment take priority now.

    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Continue the technique lesson or invent a dose before calling for help.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Call the clinical team immediately. Report worsening breathlessness, speaking difficulty and the reported lack of medicine effect.

    Why this helps¶

    Urgent assessment takes priority over completing the teaching session.
