    English meaning

    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Call the test abnormal because Esra asked for repetition.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Acknowledge that you gave too many instructions together. Follow the examiner's sequence one step at a time. Check each instruction is understood before interpreting the response.

    Why this helps¶

    The unclear sequence explains Esra's question. Simplifying communication improves the task without treating a request for clarification as a neurological finding.

    Say it to a colleague

    Sagen Sie, dass es zu viele Anweisungen gleichzeitig waren. Folgen Sie der vorgesehenen Untersuchung Schritt für Schritt. Prüfen Sie Verständnis, bevor Sie die Reaktion bewerten.

    Acknowledge that you gave too many instructions together. Follow the examiner's sequence one step at a time. Check each instruction is understood before interpreting the response.
