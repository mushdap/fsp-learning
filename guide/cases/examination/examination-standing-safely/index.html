    Patient: Beim Aufstehen bin ich unsicher.

    I feel unsteady when standing up.

    Dr Daniyal

    Das geben wir weiter und prüfen mit dem Team, wie wir Sie sicher untersuchen.

    English meaning

    We will pass that on and check with the team how to examine you safely.

    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Ask Hugo to walk alone so you can see what happens.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Ask Hugo to wait. Coordinate an appropriate supported assessment with the team. Record reported unsteadiness separately from gait findings that have not yet been examined.

    Why this helps¶

    Hugo is trying to help, but an unplanned demonstration may create risk. His report is useful without requiring him to walk alone.
