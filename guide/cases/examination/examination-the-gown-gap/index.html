    Lotte Frey · 62

    What needs to be uncovered?

    Lotte is willing to be examined but does not know how much clothing to remove. She wants privacy while changing and wants other areas covered. Explain which body areas the planned examination requires before asking her to change.

    Lotte möchte sich untersuchen lassen. Sie weiß nicht, was sie ausziehen muss. Beim Umziehen will sie Privatsphäre. Andere Körperstellen sollen bedeckt bleiben. Erklären Sie vorher den notwendigen Umfang.

    Three clear stepsDrei klare Schritte

    1. 1Explain which areas need examinationNötige Untersuchungsbereiche erklären
    2. 2Provide privacy for changingPrivatsphäre zum Umziehen schaffen
    3. 3Keep other areas covered where possibleAndere Bereiche möglichst bedeckt halten
    RememberMerken

    Explain necessary exposure and protect the other areas.

    Nötiges Freimachen erklären und andere Bereiche schützen.

    German words · human meaning

    Try these words

    Listen to the patient. Ask one clear question at a time. Dr Palwasha is a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Muss ich wirklich alles ausziehen?

    English meaning

    Do I really have to remove everything?

    Dr Palwasha

    Ich erkläre, welche Bereiche untersucht werden und welche bedeckt bleiben können.

    English meaning

    I will explain which areas will be examined and which can remain covered.

    Patient

    Ich möchte mich erst in Ruhe umziehen.

    English meaning

    I would like to change in privacy first.

    Dr Palwasha

    Natürlich. Wir sorgen für Sichtschutz und besprechen, ob Sie eine Begleitperson wünschen.

    English meaning

    Of course. We will provide privacy and discuss whether you would like a chaperone.

    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Ask her to remove everything because it is easier for staff.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Explain which areas need examination and which can stay covered. Provide private changing and discuss a chaperone. Confirm Lotte understands before proceeding.

    Why this helps¶

    Lotte needs clear limits, not just a gown. Knowing the purpose and extent of exposure helps her make an informed choice about the examination.
