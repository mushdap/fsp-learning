    The belt is not a laboratory result

    Nils says he has lost “about a stone, perhaps three kilos” since swallowing became uncomfortable. His belt is two holes tighter, but the household scales have been used mainly to weigh parcels for his online bookshop. He also mentions that he stopped buying lunch because meals take too long. The referral says weight loss without a figure. You need a useful account for colleagues without converting a vivid everyday description into a precise measurement that nobody actually made.

    Meet your patient
    Nils Hartmann, 60
    Practise
    Separate measured weight change from impression
    Scroll to begin ↓
    German words · human meaning

    Pull up a chair. Listen first.

    Nils says he has lost “about a stone, perhaps three kilos” since swallowing became uncomfortable. Dr Daniyal appears as a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Am Gürtel merke ich es, gewogen habe ich mich nicht.

    English meaning

    I notice it from my belt; I have not weighed myself.

    Dr Daniyal

    Seit wann sitzt der Gürtel lockerer?

    English meaning

    Since when has the belt been looser?

    Patient

    Seit ungefähr einem Monat, seit ich weniger esse.

    English meaning

    For about a month, since I started eating less.

    Dr Daniyal

    Ich notiere das als Ihre Beobachtung, nicht als gemessenen Wert.

    English meaning

    I will record that as your observation, not a measured value.

    Practice misstep

    The conversation gets stuck

    Practice misstep: Convert two belt holes into an exact kilogram loss.

    Better approach

    Make room for understanding

    Clarify the timeframe, any reliable earlier weight and how eating has changed. Preserve the belt observation as reported information, with the amount unmeasured. Request appropriate current assessment rather than inventing a baseline. Explore whether reduced intake reflects sticking, pain, appetite or another barrier; those explanations should not be silently merged.

    What changed—and why it helps¶

    Everyday clues can be valuable without being numerical. A tighter belt may prompt further assessment, but it cannot establish an exact weight loss. Keeping estimated, measured and unknown information separate lets the next clinician interpret the story accurately. It also avoids making Nils feel he must produce a precise answer to be believed.

    Your turn

    Nils reports a looser belt without any weight measurements. How should you record the change?

    Read the explanation

    Best next move: Document unmeasured change and clarify intake and timeframe

    The observation deserves attention even without a number. Inventing a conversion creates false precision, while dismissing it loses a potentially important change. Record the patient’s description, the period involved and the missing measurement. Ask what has made meals difficult instead of assuming that appetite alone explains the change.

    A language-practice handover

    Same person. A colleague needs the key details.

    Herr Hartmann berichtet über seit etwa einem Monat lockereren Gürtel und reduzierte Nahrungsaufnahme bei Schluckbeschwerden. Eine Gewichtsabnahme ist nicht gemessen; Ausmaß und genaue Ursache der verminderten Aufnahme sind noch zu klären.
