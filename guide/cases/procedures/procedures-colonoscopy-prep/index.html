    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Let him choose his favourite steps from both sheets.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Check the current appointment instructions with the endoscopy team. Report exactly what Gregor has already done. Let the team resolve preparation and medicine questions individually.

    Why this helps¶

    Old instructions can still look official. Mixing them may produce a plan that nobody prescribed for this appointment.

    Best next move: Verify the current plan and report completed stepsAktuellen Plan prüfen und durchgeführte Schritte melden

    • Verify the current plan and report completed stepsAktuellen Plan prüfen und durchgeführte Schritte melden

      The team needs both intended and actual preparation.Das Team braucht geplante und tatsächliche Vorbereitung.

    • Choose the older plan because he used it beforeWegen früherer Anwendung den alten Plan wählen

      Earlier use does not make the old plan current.Frühere Anwendung macht den alten Plan nicht aktuell.

    • Combine the remaining steps into a new scheduleÜbrige Schritte zu einem neuen Zeitplan verbinden

      A combined schedule would be an unverified new plan.Ein Mischplan wäre ein ungeprüfter neuer Plan.
