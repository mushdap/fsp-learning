    Two instruction sheets and one confused fridge

    Gregor has a colonoscopy appointment and two different preparation sheets, one from a previous examination. Both have been attached to the fridge with equal authority. He has combined the parts that seem easiest and asks whether that is acceptable. You need to identify the current prescribed preparation and have the endoscopy team resolve discrepancies, including medicine questions. Do not invent a universal fasting or fluid schedule from memory. The fridge may hold both documents, but only the verified plan should guide this appointment.

    Meet your patient
    Gregor Feld, 71
    Practise
    Reconcile conflicting procedure-preparation instructions

    German words · human meaning

    Pull up a chair. Listen first.

    Gregor has a colonoscopy appointment and two different preparation sheets, one from a previous examination. Dr Palwasha appears as a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Auf dem alten Blatt steht etwas anderes. Welches gilt?

    English meaning

    The old sheet says something different. Which one applies?

    Dr Palwasha

    Wir prüfen die aktuelle Anweisung für Ihren Termin mit dem Endoskopieteam.

    English meaning

    We will check the current instruction for your appointment with the endoscopy team.

    Patient

    Ich habe die beiden Pläne gemischt.

    English meaning

    I mixed the two plans.

    Dr Palwasha

    Bitte ändern Sie jetzt nicht weiter auf eigene Faust; wir klären die Abweichungen gezielt.

    English meaning

    Please do not make further changes on your own; we will clarify the discrepancies specifically.

    Practice misstep

    The conversation gets stuck

    Practice misstep: Combine whichever preparation steps you prefer.

    Better approach

    Make room for understanding

    Identify the current appointment instructions and tell the endoscopy team exactly what Gregor has already done. Resolve discrepancies individually rather than creating a new preparation schedule in the conversation.

    What changed—and why it helps¶

    Old instructions can look just as official as current ones. Version checking matters because a blended plan may match neither the requested preparation nor the patient’s present circumstances.

    Best next move: Verify the current plan and report what was already followed.

    The first choice lets the responsible team respond to the actual situation. Past success does not validate an old plan today, and a compromise may be an entirely unprescribed regimen.
