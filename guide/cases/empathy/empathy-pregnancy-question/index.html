    A private question with a clear reason

    Lea is discussing a possible imaging examination with her partner sitting beside her. The learner needs to ask whether pregnancy is possible but is unsure how to introduce the subject. A blunt assumption based on age, relationship status or appearance would be unhelpful. Lea also may not want to discuss personal details in front of her partner. The task is to explain why the question matters, offer a private conversation and leave the imaging decision to an individual benefit-risk assessment rather than repeating a blanket rule that all imaging is forbidden.

    Meet your patient
    Lea Winter, 27
    Practise

    German words · human meaning

    Pull up a chair. Listen first.

    Before a proposed investigation, a clinician needs relevant pregnancy information. Dr Palwasha appears as a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Warum fragen Sie nach einer möglichen Schwangerschaft?

    English meaning

    Why are you asking about a possible pregnancy?

    Dr Palwasha

    Das kann für die Auswahl und Planung mancher Untersuchungen wichtig sein. Möchten Sie kurz allein sprechen?

    English meaning

    That can matter for selecting and planning some examinations. Would you like to speak privately for a moment?

    Patient

    Ja, lieber ohne meinen Partner.

    English meaning

    Yes, preferably without my partner.

    Dr Palwasha

    Natürlich. Danach klären wir die Untersuchung mit dem zuständigen Team individuell.

    English meaning

    Of course. Then we will clarify the examination individually with the responsible team.

    Practice misstep

    The conversation gets stuck

    Practice misstep: If you might be pregnant, no X-ray examination can ever be performed.

    Better approach

    Make room for understanding

    Explain the purpose of the question and offer privacy without implying a particular answer. Ask respectfully whether pregnancy is possible and ensure the information reaches the appropriate team for individual planning. Do not make a blanket ban or guarantee about imaging. The relevant clinician should discuss the specific examination’s benefits and risks.

    What changed—and why it helps¶

    A sensitive question is easier to answer when its purpose and audience are clear. Privacy should be offered rather than inferred from the relationship in the room. The answer informs a clinical decision; it does not automatically settle every possible investigation with one universal rule.

    Your turn

    What is the most appropriate approach?

    Read the explanation

    Best next move: Explain the reason, offer privacy and seek individual review

    The reason and privacy offer support an accurate, voluntary answer. A partner cannot automatically answer for Lea, and appearance cannot establish pregnancy status. The information should guide an individual examination discussion rather than a blanket claim that every imaging option is prohibited or safe.
