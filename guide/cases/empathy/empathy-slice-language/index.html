    Slice images? Will you cut something open on my body for that?

    Dr Daniyal: Nein. Das Wort beschreibt die Bilder, nicht einen Schnitt an Ihrem Körper.

    Patient: Also verschiedene Ansichten im Computer?

    So different views in the computer?

    Dr Daniyal

    Genau. Ich erkläre den konkreten Ablauf der geplanten Untersuchung noch einmal ohne diesen Fachausdruck.

    English meaning

    Exactly. I will explain the actual process of the planned examination again without that technical term.

    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Laugh at the misunderstanding or repeat the same term.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Explain that slices describe the displayed images. Describe the planned examination in everyday language.

    Why this helps¶

    The problem is unclear wording, not a lack of intelligence.
