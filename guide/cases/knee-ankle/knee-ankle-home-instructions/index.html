    The leaflet that speaks three dialects of medicine

    Felix has received an individual ankle-care plan from the treating clinician, but the printed leaflet uses unfamiliar words. He nods through the explanation, then asks whether loading means charging something. The printer has helpfully produced two faint copies and one perfectly dark blank page. Your task is not to invent a new care plan; it is to check what Felix understood from the existing one and identify questions that must return to the clinician before he leaves.

    Meet your patient
    Felix Urban, 33
    Practise
    Teach-back of an existing individual plan
    Scroll to begin ↓
    German words · human meaning

    Pull up a chair. Listen first.

    Felix has received an individual ankle-care plan from the treating clinician, but the printed leaflet uses unfamiliar words. Dr Daniyal appears as a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Was bedeutet Belastung in diesem Zettel?

    English meaning

    What does loading mean in this leaflet?

    Dr Daniyal

    Damit ist gemeint, wie viel Gewicht Sie auf das Bein geben. Was wurde Ihnen dazu gesagt?

    English meaning

    It means how much weight you put on the leg. What were you told about that?

    Patient

    Ich bin mir nicht sicher.

    English meaning

    I am not sure.

    Dr Daniyal

    Dann klären wir Ihre genaue Vorgabe mit dem Behandlungsteam, bevor Sie gehen.

    English meaning

    Then we will clarify your exact instruction with the clinical team before you leave.

    Practice misstep

    The conversation gets stuck

    Practice misstep: Just follow the leaflet; it explains everything.

    Better approach

    Make room for understanding

    Translate the unfamiliar term and ask Felix to explain his actual instruction. Resolve any uncertainty with the treating team rather than substituting a generic ankle rule.

    What changed—and why it helps¶

    Nodding is not proof that an instruction was understood. Teach-back tests the explanation and exposes missing details while there is still someone available to clarify them.

    What should you do when Felix cannot explain his loading instruction?

    Read the explanation

    Best next move: Check understanding and clarify the individual instruction.

    The second choice keeps the approved plan central while checking comprehension. Repetition without translation leaves the gap; an invented schedule replaces clinical instructions rather than explaining them.

    A language-practice handover

    Same person. A colleague needs the key details.

    Herr Urban versteht die im individuellen Plan genannte Belastungsvorgabe noch nicht sicher. Die konkrete Anweisung wird vor Verlassen der Einrichtung mit dem Behandlungsteam geklärt; es wurde kein eigenes Belastungsschema ergänzt.
