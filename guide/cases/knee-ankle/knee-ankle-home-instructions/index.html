    An unclear ankle instruction

    Felix has an individual ankle-care plan, but he does not understand the word loading. He is unsure how much weight he may put on the leg. Explain the term and clarify his exact instruction before he leaves.

    Felix hat einen persönlichen Behandlungsplan. Er versteht das Wort Belastung nicht. Wie viel Gewicht darf auf das Bein? Er weiß es nicht sicher. Klären Sie seine genaue Anweisung vor dem Heimweg.

    Three clear stepsDrei klare Schritte

    1. 1Word: loading means weight on legWort: Belastung heißt Gewicht auf dem Bein
    2. 2Ask Felix to explain his planFelix seinen Plan erklären lassen
    3. 3Clarify with the treating teamMit dem Behandlungsteam klären
    RememberMerken

    Check the individual instruction, not a general rule.

    Die persönliche Anweisung prüfen, keine allgemeine Regel einsetzen.

    German words · human meaning

    Try these words

    Listen to the patient. Ask one clear question at a time. Dr Daniyal is a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Was bedeutet Belastung in diesem Zettel?

    English meaning

    What does loading mean in this leaflet?

    Dr Daniyal

    Damit ist gemeint, wie viel Gewicht Sie auf das Bein geben. Was wurde Ihnen dazu gesagt?

    English meaning

    It means how much weight you put on the leg. What were you told about that?

    Patient

    Ich bin mir nicht sicher.

    English meaning

    I am not sure.

    Dr Daniyal

    Dann klären wir Ihre genaue Vorgabe mit dem Behandlungsteam, bevor Sie gehen.

    English meaning

    Then we will clarify your exact instruction with the clinical team before you leave.

    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Tell Felix to follow the leaflet without checking understanding.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Explain that loading means weight on the leg. Ask what he understood. If the exact instruction is unclear, check it with the treating team before he leaves.

    Why this helps¶

    Nodding does not prove understanding. Asking Felix to explain his plan shows whether the wording or the exact instruction still needs clarification.

    Your next step

    What should you do when Felix cannot explain his loading instruction?

    Read the explanation

    Best next move: Check understanding and clarify the individual instruction.

    Checking understanding keeps Felix's own plan central. Repeating jargon or inventing a loading schedule would not explain the instruction his team gave him.
