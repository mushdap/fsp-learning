    Where was Daria sitting?

    Daria's knee hit the dashboard. The record says driver, but she was the front passenger. Her knee and hip hurt. How can you correct Daria’s seating position without assigning blame?

    Dr Palwasha

    English meaning

    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Ask why Daria caused the accident.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Ask where she sat, what struck her knee and where else it hurts. Correct the driver entry. Explain that these questions help assess injuries, not assign blame.

    Why this helps¶

    The seating position explains the impact. Neutral questions make it easier for Daria to correct a factual error without defending herself.
