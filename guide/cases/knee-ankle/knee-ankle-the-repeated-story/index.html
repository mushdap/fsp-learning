    Correct the order of the fall

    Petra says her ankle turned first and then she fell onto her knee. Your summary reverses the order. She is tired of repeating the story. Acknowledge the error and check the corrected sequence with her.

    Petra knickte zuerst um. Danach fiel sie auf das Knie. Ihre Zusammenfassung nennt die umgekehrte Reihenfolge. Petra ist müde vom Wiederholen. Gestehen Sie den Fehler ein und bestätigen Sie den richtigen Ablauf.

    German words · human meaning

    Try these words

    Listen to the patient. Ask one clear question at a time. Dr Palwasha is a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Nein, zuerst bin ich umgeknickt, dann aufs Knie gefallen.

    English meaning

    No, first I twisted my ankle, then I fell onto my knee.

    Dr Palwasha

    Danke für die Korrektur. Ich hatte die Reihenfolge vertauscht.

    English meaning

    Thank you for correcting me. I had reversed the order.

    Patient

    Genau das meine ich.

    English meaning

    That is exactly what I mean.

    Dr Palwasha

    Erst das Umknicken, danach der Aufprall aufs Knie. Stimmt das jetzt?

    English meaning

    First the ankle twisted, then the impact on the knee. Is that correct now?

    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Insist that the first note is correct.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Thank Petra for correcting you. Say that you reversed the order. Repeat the corrected sequence, ask if it is right and update the note.

    Why this helps¶

    The order explains how the injury happened. Accepting the correction improves the history and avoids sending the same error to the next clinician.

    Your next step

    How should you respond when Petra corrects the injury sequence?

    Read the explanation

    Best next move: Thank her and confirm the corrected sequence.

    Thanking Petra and checking the corrected order repairs the specific mistake. Defending the note or stopping clarification would leave the wrong sequence unresolved.
