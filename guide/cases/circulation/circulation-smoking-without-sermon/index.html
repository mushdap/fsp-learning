    Uwe Seidel · 63

    Ask Uwe about smoking without blame

    Uwe has reduced his smoking but still smokes more during stress. He expects criticism and worries about getting help for his leg pain. Ask about his current pattern and offer support respectfully.

    Uwe raucht weniger als früher, bei Stress aber wieder mehr. Er erwartet Vorwürfe und sorgt sich um Hilfe bei seinen Beinschmerzen. Fragen Sie sachlich nach seinem Konsum und bieten Sie Unterstützung an.

    See the differenceDen Unterschied erkennen

    1. 1Before: smoked moreFrüher: mehr geraucht
    2. 2Now: less, but more during stressJetzt: weniger, bei Stress aber mehr
    3. 3Next: clarify use and offer supportDanach: Konsum klären und Hilfe anbieten
    RememberMerken

    Ask clearly and offer support without blame.

    Fragen Sie klar und bieten Sie Hilfe ohne Vorwurf an.

    German words · human meaning

    Try these words

    Listen to the patient. Ask one clear question at a time. Dr Palwasha is a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Ich rauche noch. Die übliche Predigt kenne ich schon.

    English meaning

    I still smoke. I know the usual sermon already.

    Dr Palwasha

    Ich möchte Sie nicht verurteilen. Wie hat sich die Menge verändert?

    English meaning

    I do not want to judge you. How has the amount changed?

    Patient

    Weniger als früher, aber bei Stress wird es wieder mehr.

    English meaning

    Less than before, but during stress it increases again.

    Dr Palwasha

    Möchten Sie besprechen, welche Unterstützung für Sie passen könnte?

    English meaning

    Would you like to discuss what support might suit you?

    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Blame Uwe for continuing to smoke.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Ask about current and earlier smoking. Acknowledge his reduction and ask whether he wants to discuss support.

    Why this helps¶

    Neutral questions make it easier to give an honest history.

    Where this exercise comes from¶

    Fictional practice case. The cited physical PDF pages inspire leg-pain history, arterial circulation concerns and patient questions about assessment. This original fictional exercise focuses on discuss smoking neutrally and ask permission before offering support. The patient, setting, dialogue and decision are invented; no clinical outcome or exam prediction is claimed.

    Topic reference: FSP in Bayern 2024, Shamil Gurbanov, second edition, supplied PDF physical page(s) 14, 16. Physical page numbers count from the first PDF page; printed numbers are usually three lower. Dialogue, names and teaching choices are original adaptations.
