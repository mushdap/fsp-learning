    A short handover with unknowns

    Fiona arrives after a road collision with chest and abdominal pain. Her medicines and allergies are unconfirmed. What belongs in Fiona’s brief handover when medicines are unknown?

    English meaning

    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Write no medicines and no allergies because none were mentioned.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Report the collision and current chest and abdominal pain first. Say that medicines and allergies remain unconfirmed. Keep the handover brief so assessment can continue.

    Why this helps¶

    Information not yet asked is not a negative answer. The team can use a short report that clearly identifies missing facts.
