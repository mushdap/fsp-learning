    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Assume that hearing spleen means a confirmed rupture.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Ask what Lara understood. Arrange a review of actual findings with the responsible clinician. Separate confirmed results from open questions and explain one point at a time.

    Why this helps¶

    Remembering one word does not reveal the result. The actual report is needed to explain what was found and what remains uncertain.

    • Infer injury from the overheard wordAus dem gehörten Wort eine Verletzung ableiten

      An isolated word does not establish a finding.Ein einzelnes Wort bestätigt keinen Befund.

      The responsible clinician can separate findings from open questions.Die zuständige Fachperson trennt Befunde von offenen Fragen.

    • No report means no injuryAus fehlendem Bericht auf keine Verletzung schließen
