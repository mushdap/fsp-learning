    Make time to hear Nora

    Work pressure alone does not establish the cause of her headaches.

    Read the explanation

    Best next move: Agree on a pause, then resume her historyEine Pause vereinbaren, dann die Anamnese fortsetzen

    • Agree on a pause, then resume her historyEine Pause vereinbaren, dann die Anamnese fortsetzen

      A short agreed pause creates space to hear her symptoms.Eine kurze vereinbarte Pause schafft Raum für ihre Beschwerden.

    • Continue questioning while she answers work messagesWeiterfragen, während sie ihre Arbeitsnachrichten beantwortet

      Divided attention makes important details easier to miss.Bei geteilter Aufmerksamkeit gehen wichtige Angaben leichter verloren.

    • Postpone all history questions until a quieter appointment tomorrowAlle Fragen auf einen ruhigeren Termin morgen verschieben

      She wants time to talk now. First try a practical pause instead of postponing the whole history.Sie möchte jetzt sprechen. Versuchen Sie zunächst eine kurze Pause, statt die ganze Anamnese zu verschieben.

    Where this exercise comes from¶

    Fictional practice case. The cited physical PDF pages inspire FSP patient conversation, accurate documentation and professional handover. This original fictional exercise focuses on negotiate a brief agenda and manage interruptions respectfully. The patient, setting, dialogue and decision are invented; no clinical outcome or exam prediction is claimed.

    Topic reference: FSP in Bayern 2024, Shamil Gurbanov, second edition, supplied PDF physical page(s) 7, 8. Physical page numbers count from the first PDF page; printed numbers are usually three lower. Dialogue, names and teaching choices are original adaptations.
