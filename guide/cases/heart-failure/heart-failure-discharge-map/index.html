    The plan has to reach the fourth floor

    Erika’s draft plan says “home with follow-up.” It sounds pleasantly complete until she asks how she will reach her fourth-floor flat while the lift is being repaired. Her daughter can bring shopping on Sundays but cannot stay during the week. Nobody has made a discharge decision in this scene; they are checking what a future plan would require. The learner must turn broad words into practical questions without promising transport or home services that have not been arranged. A plan should travel farther than the printer tray.

    Meet your patient
    Erika Lenz, 69
    Practise
    Translate a proposed home plan into verified practical arrangements.

    German words · human meaning

    Pull up a chair. Listen first.

    The team discusses a possible future home plan after assessment. Dr Daniyal appears as a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Der Aufzug ist kaputt. Wie soll ich nach oben kommen?

    English meaning

    The lift is broken. How am I supposed to get upstairs?

    Dr Daniyal

    Das müssen wir in die Planung aufnehmen. Welche Hilfe ist tatsächlich verfügbar?

    English meaning

    We need to include that in planning. What help is actually available?

    Patient

    Meine Tochter nur sonntags.

    English meaning

    My daughter only on Sundays.

    Dr Daniyal

    Dann klären wir die offenen Punkte mit dem Team, bevor wir von einer gesicherten Lösung sprechen.

    English meaning

    Then we will clarify the open points with the team before calling it an assured solution.

    Practice misstep

    The conversation gets stuck

    Practice misstep: Your daughter exists, so home support is sorted.

    Better approach

    Make room for understanding

    Ask about stairs, transport, daily tasks and the actual availability of help. Bring the gaps into the team’s planning before calling the arrangement complete. Separate a proposed discharge plan from a clinical decision and confirmed services. Give Erika a clear account of what is being checked rather than a blanket assurance that everything will be organised.

    What changed—and why it helps¶

    A short phrase such as “home with support” can hide several unanswered questions. Concrete planning protects against assuming that family presence equals daily availability. The learner’s role is to make the missing pieces visible so the team can assess feasibility and communicate a plan that exists outside the note.

    Best next move: Practical gaps remain in a proposed plan

    The scene establishes practical gaps, not a completed arrangement or a permanent impossibility. Stating them clearly lets the team explore options. The learner should avoid promising a service or announcing a discharge decision that has not been made, while ensuring Erika’s actual living situation is heard.
