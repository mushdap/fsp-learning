    English meaning

    The cause is still unclear. The deterioration needs immediate assessment.

    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Assume the new breathing difficulty is only his usual symptom.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Call the clinical team immediately. Report the observed speaking difficulty and the patient's account of worsening breathing.

    Why this helps¶

    A severe new change needs immediate assessment even after longstanding symptoms.
