    Plan for pain during dressing changes

    Marlene remembers pain when her last dressing was removed. She stayed silent because the team looked busy. Before today's change, agree how she can report pain and ask for a pause.

    Marlene hatte beim letzten Entfernen des Verbandes Schmerzen. Weil das Team beschäftigt wirkte, sagte sie nichts. Besprechen Sie vor dem heutigen Wechsel, wie sie Schmerzen melden und um eine Pause bitten kann.

    German words · human meaning

    Try these words

    Listen to the patient. Ask one clear question at a time. Dr Palwasha is a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Beim letzten Wechsel tat es sehr weh.

    English meaning

    The last dressing change hurt a lot.

    Dr Palwasha

    Danke, dass Sie das sagen. Was war besonders schmerzhaft?

    English meaning

    Thank you for telling me. What was particularly painful?

    Patient

    Als der Verband abgelöst wurde. Ich habe nichts gesagt.

    English meaning

    When the dressing was removed. I did not say anything.

    Dr Palwasha

    Bitte sagen Sie sofort Bescheid. Wir besprechen vorher, wie wir Schmerzen berücksichtigen und pausieren können.

    English meaning

    Please tell us immediately. We will discuss beforehand how to address pain and pause if needed.

    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Ask her to endure the change without interrupting.

    A clearer answer

    Ask what hurt last time. Agree on reporting pain and pausing, with the responsible team involved.

    Professional German example

    Where this exercise comes from¶

    Fictional practice case. The cited physical PDF pages inspire leg-ulcer history, dressing pain, topical reactions, documentation and practical care. This original fictional exercise focuses on prepare for discomfort with consent and a clear pause signal. The patient, setting, dialogue and decision are invented; no clinical outcome or exam prediction is claimed.

    Topic reference: FSP in Bayern 2024, Shamil Gurbanov, second edition, supplied PDF physical page(s) 33, 35. Physical page numbers count from the first PDF page; printed numbers are usually three lower. Dialogue, names and teaching choices are original adaptations.
