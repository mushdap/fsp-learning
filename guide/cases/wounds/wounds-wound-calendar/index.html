    Three dressings, one impossible week

    Henrik nods at a plan for repeated visits, then studies the bus timetable on his phone. His shift starts before the first bus reaches the clinic, and a taxi each time would consume his food budget. He has not said no; he has said yes to a plan he cannot carry out. The learner could record “follow-up arranged” and move on. A better conversation checks the journey, work constraints and available services before assuming the timetable on the clinic wall has become a realistic plan in Henrik’s life.

    Meet your patient
    Henrik Rose, 60
    Practise
    Check feasibility of follow-up and distinguish proposed from arranged care.

    German words · human meaning

    Pull up a chair. Listen first.

    Care planning for repeated wound reviews conflicts with work and transport. Dr Palwasha appears as a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Mit dem Bus schaffe ich diese Uhrzeit nicht.

    English meaning

    I cannot make that time by bus.

    Dr Palwasha

    Gut, dass Sie das sagen. Welche Zeiten und Wege wären für Sie möglich?

    English meaning

    It is good that you say so. What times and travel options would be possible for you?

    Patient

    Nachmittags eher, aber ich weiß nicht, ob das angeboten wird.

    English meaning

    Afternoons would be easier, but I do not know whether that is offered.

    Dr Palwasha

    Wir klären die Möglichkeiten mit dem Team, bevor wir den Plan als vereinbart festhalten.

    English meaning

    We will clarify the options with the team before recording the plan as agreed.

    Practice misstep

    The conversation gets stuck

    Practice misstep: The appointment is available, so attendance is your responsibility.

    Better approach

    Make room for understanding

    Ask how Henrik will get to the visits and what constraints affect attendance. Explore alternatives with the responsible service without promising availability. Record whether a time is proposed, booked or still being clarified. Ensure he knows whom to contact if the arrangement fails, rather than treating a polite nod as a workable agreement.

    What changed—and why it helps¶

    A clinical plan has to cross a real town on a real timetable. Transport and work are part of whether it can happen. Clarifying them early helps avoid a later blame story about missed visits when the obstacle was visible from the beginning but nobody asked.

    Your turn

    What should the record reflect now?

    Read the explanation

    Best next move: Plan needs transport and scheduling clarification

    The plan remains under clarification because the proposed timing is not feasible. Recording it as arranged hides the barrier, while calling it refusal misrepresents Henrik’s willingness. The next useful action is coordination with the service, with the status of that coordination made clear to everyone involved.
