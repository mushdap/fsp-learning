    The miracle cream has a missing label

    Bernd bought a cream after a neighbour called it “marvellous.” The tube now has a folded end, a rubbed-off label and a popularity rating of one enthusiastic neighbour. His skin became itchy and red after using it, but he is unsure whether the wound itself was already worsening. The learner must separate timing from assumed cause and identify the product if possible. This is not the moment to blame Bernd for trying to help himself or confidently label every new red patch as an allergy.

    Meet your patient
    Bernd Hase, 64
    Practise
    Clarify a possible topical reaction while preserving uncertainty.

    German words · human meaning

    Pull up a chair. Listen first.

    A wound history reveals irritation after a shop-bought cream. Dr Palwasha appears as a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Nach der Salbe hat es gejuckt und wurde rot.

    English meaning

    After the cream it itched and became red.

    Dr Palwasha

    Wann begann das im Verhältnis zum Auftragen?

    English meaning

    When did that start in relation to applying it?

    Patient

    Am selben Abend. Den Namen kann ich nicht mehr lesen.

    English meaning

    That same evening. I can no longer read the name.

    Dr Palwasha

    Wir notieren den Zusammenhang und versuchen, das Präparat zu identifizieren.

    English meaning

    We will record the timing and try to identify the product.

    Practice misstep

    The conversation gets stuck

    Practice misstep: That is definitely an allergy to all wound creams.

    Better approach

    Make room for understanding

    Ask about the product, timing, location and type of reaction, and whether other changes occurred. Document a suspected association rather than a confirmed cause. Seek clinical review of the wound and skin, and use packaging or pharmacy information to identify the cream when possible. Do not generalise to every similar product.

    What changed—and why it helps¶

    Events that occur together may be connected, but timing alone does not prove the mechanism. A precise description helps the reviewing clinician consider irritation, allergy or another cause. Keeping the product identity and uncertainty visible is more useful than a broad label that future clinicians cannot interpret.

    Which note is most accurate?

    Read the explanation

    Best next move: Itching and redness after an unidentified cream

    The accurate note keeps the reported symptoms and timing without inventing the product or mechanism. A blanket allergy label is unsupported, while ignoring the event because the label is unreadable loses useful information. Identifying the cream and assessing the skin are the next clarification tasks.
