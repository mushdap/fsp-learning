    Jonas Krug · 46

    What is known about the father's illness?

    Jonas knows his father had bowel surgery. Relatives disagree about whether it was cancer or inflammation. Jonas fears the same illness. Record the known operation and the uncertain diagnosis separately.

    Jonas weiß, dass sein Vater am Darm operiert wurde. Verwandte nennen Krebs oder eine Entzündung. Jonas hat Angst vor derselben Krankheit. Trennen Sie die berichtete Operation von der unklaren Diagnose.

    See the differenceDen Unterschied erkennen

    1. 1Reported: father had bowel surgeryBerichtet: Vater hatte eine Darmoperation
    2. 2Unknown: reason and age at diagnosisUnbekannt: Ursache und Alter bei Diagnose
    3. 3Next: seek reliable detailsDanach: verlässliche Angaben suchen

    Keep uncertainty in the family history.

    Halten Sie Unsicherheit in der Familienanamnese fest.

    German words · human meaning

    Try these words

    Listen to the patient. Ask one clear question at a time. Dr Daniyal is a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Mein Vater hatte vielleicht Darmkrebs. Bekomme ich das auch?

    English meaning

    My father may have had bowel cancer. Will I get it too?

    Dr Daniyal

    Die genaue Erkrankung ist noch unklar. Was wissen Sie sicher darüber?

    English meaning

    The exact illness is still unclear. What do you know for certain about it?

    Patient

    Nur, dass er operiert wurde.

    English meaning

    Only that he had an operation.

    Dr Daniyal

    Ich notiere das so und bespreche mit Ihnen, welche Angaben wir klären können.

    English meaning

    I will record it that way and discuss which details we can clarify.

    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Write confirmed bowel cancer in the family history.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Record the reported bowel operation and uncertain cause. Discuss how the family history could be clarified.

    Why this helps¶

    An uncertain family report is not a confirmed diagnosis.

    Fictional practice case. The cited physical PDF pages inspire general history, medication, allergy, social and family questions. This original fictional exercise focuses on attribute uncertain family diagnoses and respond to inherited-risk fears. The patient, setting, dialogue and decision are invented; no clinical outcome or exam prediction is claimed.

    Topic reference: FSP in Bayern 2024, Shamil Gurbanov, second edition, supplied PDF physical page(s) 9. Physical page numbers count from the first PDF page; printed numbers are usually three lower. Dialogue, names and teaching choices are original adaptations.
