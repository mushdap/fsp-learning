    Vera Stahl · 58

    Can Vera use her written plan?

    Vera understands only the first line of her written care plan. She does not know whom to call if symptoms worsen. Review the actual instructions and ask her to explain what she would do.

    Vera versteht nur die erste Zeile ihres Versorgungsplans. Sie weiß nicht, wen sie bei Verschlechterung anrufen soll. Gehen Sie die echten Anweisungen durch. Lassen Sie Vera ihr Vorgehen erklären.

    German words · human meaning

    Try these words

    Listen to the patient. Ask one clear question at a time. Dr Daniyal is a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Den ersten Satz verstehe ich, aber wen rufe ich bei einer Verschlechterung an?

    English meaning

    I understand the first sentence, but whom do I call if things worsen?

    Dr Daniyal

    Schauen wir die konkreten Kontaktangaben und Warnzeichen in Ihrem Plan gemeinsam an.

    English meaning

    Let us look together at the specific contacts and warning signs in your plan.

    Patient

    Ich möchte sicher sein, dass ich die richtige Nummer benutze.

    English meaning

    I want to be sure I use the right number.

    Dr Daniyal

    Bitte sagen Sie anschließend in Ihren Worten, wie Sie vorgehen würden. Offene Fragen klären wir mit dem Team.

    English meaning

    Please then tell me in your own words what you would do. We will clarify open questions with the team.

    Where this exercise comes from¶

    Fictional practice case. The cited physical PDF pages inspire respiratory infection symptoms, pneumonia and tonsillitis assessment conversations. This original fictional exercise focuses on check usable understanding of an existing plan without creating a new one. The patient, setting, dialogue and decision are invented; no clinical outcome or exam prediction is claimed.

    Topic reference: FSP in Bayern 2024, Shamil Gurbanov, second edition, supplied PDF physical page(s) 53, 55. Physical page numbers count from the first PDF page; printed numbers are usually three lower. Dialogue, names and teaching choices are original adaptations.
