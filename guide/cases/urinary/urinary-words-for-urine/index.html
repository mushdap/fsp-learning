    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Mark burning, frequent urination, urgency and low volume as present.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Ask one short question at a time. Separate burning, frequency, urgency and amount. Record confirmed answers and keep unanswered questions open.

    Why this helps¶

    A general phrase can describe different symptoms. Separate questions prevent an unclear complaint from becoming several symptoms the patient never confirmed.

    • Enter burning, frequency and urgency as presentBrennen, Häufigkeit und Harndrang als vorhanden eintragen

      Only burning has been confirmed.Nur Brennen ist bestätigt.

    • Ask about each symptom separatelyJedes Symptom einzeln erfragen

      Separate questions prevent unsupported additions.Einzelne Fragen verhindern unbelegte Ergänzungen.
