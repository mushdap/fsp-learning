    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Use Sofia's drawing to clarify where pain began, where it travels and when it changed. Ask about other symptoms. Keep the cause open until assessment.

    Why this helps¶

    Pointing can describe symptoms clearly without medical words. It gives useful history, but does not replace the rest of the assessment.

    So lässt es sich fachlich ausdrückenA professional way to express this

    Eine Ausstrahlung in Richtung Leiste wird vermutet, nicht sicher angegeben.Pain travelling towards the groin is suggested, not stated with certainty.
