    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Call the tingling normal and tell her to keep waiting.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Thank Elena and arrange prompt clinical reassessment. Report that tingling started only a few minutes ago. Update the note instead of copying unchanged.

    Why this helps¶

    The new symptom changes the waiting history. Its start time helps the team distinguish the earlier complaint from the current situation.
