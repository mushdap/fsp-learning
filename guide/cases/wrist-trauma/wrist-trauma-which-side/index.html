    The wrist changed sides on paper

    Tobias slipped beside his bicycle and landed on his right hand. The referral says left wrist, while he keeps protecting the right one. He jokes that the form must have used a mirror. He also mentions a sore area on his chest from the handlebar. You are about to summarise the history for a colleague. This is the moment to check the mismatch with Tobias, not choose whichever side appears in the more official-looking document or quietly forget the second injury.

    Meet your patient
    Tobias Kern, 44
    Practise
    Resolve conflicting laterality before handover
    Scroll to begin ↓
    German words · human meaning

    Pull up a chair. Listen first.

    Tobias slipped beside his bicycle and landed on his right hand. Dr Daniyal appears as a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Es ist die rechte Hand, nicht die linke.

    English meaning

    It is the right hand, not the left.

    Dr Daniyal

    Danke. Ich gleiche das mit der Überweisung ab. Wo tut es außerdem weh?

    English meaning

    Thank you. I will reconcile that with the referral. Where else does it hurt?

    Patient

    Hier am Brustkorb.

    English meaning

    Here on my chest.

    Dr Daniyal

    Ich gebe beide Beschwerden weiter und korrigiere die Seitenangabe nachvollziehbar.

    English meaning

    I will pass on both complaints and correct the side transparently.

    Practice misstep

    The conversation gets stuck

    Practice misstep: The referral says left; that must be correct.

    Better approach

    Make room for understanding

    Confirm the painful side directly, reconcile the referral and include the chest complaint. Make the correction visible rather than silently replacing the original account.

    Hear the whole accident story

    A copied side error can travel farther than the patient. Checking the discrepancy also creates space for an associated injury that the wrist label missed.

    Read the explanation

    Best next move: Confirm the side and include both injury sites.

    The second option repairs the discrepancy and preserves the associated complaint. Neither a printed form nor pain ranking justifies discarding what Tobias reports.
