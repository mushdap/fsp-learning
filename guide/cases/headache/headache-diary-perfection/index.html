    The diary does not need a design award

    Milo was advised to record headaches and arrived with a spreadsheet containing seventeen colours, six tabs and no entries after Tuesday. He spent longer designing it than observing the symptoms. He is attending a review of recurrent headaches and wants to know what information would actually help. The communication task is to agree a simple, achievable way of noting episodes and relevant context with the treating team, rather than turning the diary into another demanding task or assuming every coinciding event is a proven trigger.

    Meet your patient
    Milo Schenk, 35
    Practise
    Create a manageable symptom diary conversation

    German words · human meaning

    Pull up a chair. Listen first.

    Milo was advised to record headaches and arrived with a spreadsheet containing seventeen colours, six tabs and no entries after Tuesday. Dr Daniyal appears as a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Ich habe die Tabelle vorbereitet, aber kaum ausgefüllt.

    English meaning

    I prepared the spreadsheet but hardly filled it in.

    Dr Daniyal

    Welche wenigen Angaben könnten Sie im Alltag zuverlässig notieren?

    English meaning

    Which few details could you reliably record in daily life?

    Patient

    Beginn, Dauer und was ich genommen habe.

    English meaning

    Onset, duration and what I took.

    Dr Daniyal

    Das ist ein guter Ausgangspunkt; wir stimmen die wichtigsten Punkte mit dem Team ab.

    English meaning

    That is a useful starting point; we will agree the key items with the team.

    Practice misstep

    The conversation gets stuck

    Practice misstep: Require every cell before offering further discussion.

    Better approach

    Make room for understanding

    Agree a small set of useful observations with the treating team and Milo, such as timing, duration, impact and medicine use. Choose a format he can maintain. Explain that patterns may suggest questions but do not automatically prove triggers. Review the diary as an aid to conversation, not a condition for being believed or receiving care.

    See the communication skill

    A useful diary beats a perfect diary

    Too much structure can prevent any observation from happening. A manageable record is more useful than a perfect empty dashboard. The diary should support recall and shared assessment, not make Milo responsible for diagnosing himself. Ordinary gaps can be discussed honestly instead of filled later with guessed details to make the chart look complete.

    A small decision

    Your turn

    Milo’s elaborate headache diary is mostly empty. What would make it more useful?

    Read the explanation

    Best next move: Agree a small usable record and review it together

    The useful move makes the task achievable and keeps interpretation cautious. An incomplete diary is not a reason to withhold attention. Coincidence does not establish a trigger. Choose relevant observations, discuss gaps openly and use the record alongside clinical assessment rather than as a substitute for it.
