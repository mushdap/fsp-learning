    Tom Berger · 38

    What Tom remembers and what his partner saw

    Tom remembers a cup, then voices calling him. His partner saw stiffening and jerking during the gap. Tom cannot remember the movements his partner saw. How should you combine their accounts?

    Tom erinnert sich an eine Tasse, dann an Stimmen, die ihn riefen. Seine Partnerin sah während der Lücke Versteifung und Zuckungen. Tom erinnert Bewegungen nicht, seine Partnerin hat sie gesehen. Wie dokumentieren Sie?

    Record who remembers or observed each part.

    Notieren Sie, wer welchen Teil erinnert oder beobachtete.

    German words · human meaning

    Try these words

    Listen to the patient. Ask one clear question at a time. Dr Palwasha is a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    An die Zuckungen erinnere ich mich nicht.

    English meaning

    I do not remember the jerking.

    Dr Palwasha

    Das notiere ich so. Dürfen wir Ihre Partnerin nach ihrer Beobachtung fragen?

    English meaning

    I will record that. May we ask your partner what she observed?

    Patient

    Ja, sie hat den Ablauf gesehen.

    English meaning

    Yes, she saw what happened.

    Dr Palwasha

    Wir trennen Ihre Erinnerung und ihren Bericht und kennzeichnen geschätzte Zeiten.

    English meaning

    We will separate your memory and her account and mark estimated times.

    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Mistake: Record no jerking because he cannot remember it.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Ask Tom about before and after the memory gap. With permission, ask his partner what she observed, including recovery. Separate estimates from measured times. Do not treat missing memory as proof that movements did not happen.

    Why this helps¶

    The patient and witness can describe different parts of one event. Combining them does not require inventing a shared memory.
