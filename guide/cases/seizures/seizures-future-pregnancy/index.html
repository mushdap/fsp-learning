    Plan treatment before pregnancy

    Sophie has epilepsy and may want a baby next year. Online medicine information makes her consider stopping treatment. Sophie considers stopping epilepsy medicine while planning pregnancy. What should you communicate?

    Sophie hat Epilepsie und möchte vielleicht nächstes Jahr ein Kind. Onlineinformationen lassen sie über einen Behandlungsstopp nachdenken. Sophie möchte wegen Kinderwunsches ihre Medikamente absetzen. Was tun Sie?

    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Mistake: Tell her to stop treatment immediately.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Arrange timely specialist advice about pregnancy and medication. Verify the current plan and earlier instructions. Explain that Sophie should not stop or change treatment independently. Discuss worries without prescribing alternatives or promising risk-free outcomes.

    Why this helps¶

    Planning early allows individual advice before a rushed decision. Fearful reading should lead to a review, not an unsupported medication change.
