    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Mistake: Identify the insulin from pen colour alone.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Read the labels and compare them with the verified prescription. Ask Selin what she actually used without suggesting names. Mark unresolved details and contact the prescribing team. Do not infer dose or timing from colour.

    Why this helps¶

    A colour is not a reliable medicine identity. Agreement with a suggested name does not show that the patient remembers it.

    Everyday words → professional wording¶

    Ein Satz, zwei AusdrucksweisenOne sentence, two ways to say it

    Patientenangabe · Gesprächszeile 1Patient account · Dialogue line 1

    Das blaue war morgens, glaube ich.The blue one was for mornings, I think.

    So lässt es sich fachlich ausdrückenA professional way to express this

    Das blaue Gerät wird unsicher der morgendlichen Anwendung zugeordnet; das Präparat ist damit nicht identifiziert.The blue device is tentatively associated with morning use; this does not identify the product.

    MerkwortKey phrase: Unsichere PräparatezuordnungUncertain medicine identification

    Die Information bleibt gleich. Keine weiteren Befunde ergänzen.Keep the same information. Do not add further findings.
