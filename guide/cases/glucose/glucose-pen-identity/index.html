    Identify insulin by the label

    Selin brings two insulin pens to a follow-up. She describes them by colour and time of use. The labels and prescription have not been checked. Suggested names make her agree, even when she is unsure.

    Selin bringt zwei Insulinpens zum Folgetermin. Sie beschreibt Farbe und Einnahmezeit. Etiketten und Verordnung wurden noch nicht geprüft. Bei vorgeschlagenen Namen stimmt sie zu, obwohl sie unsicher ist.

    German words · human meaning

    Try these words

    Listen to the patient. Ask one clear question at a time. Dr Daniyal is a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Das blaue war morgens, glaube ich.

    English meaning

    The blue one was for mornings, I think.

    Dr Daniyal

    Ich möchte den Namen nicht aus der Farbe ableiten. Dürfen wir das Etikett prüfen?

    English meaning

    I do not want to infer the name from the colour. May we check the label?

    Patient

    Wenn Sie mir Namen nennen, komme ich durcheinander.

    English meaning

    If you suggest names, I get confused.

    Dr Daniyal

    Dann prüfen wir die Packungen und den verordneten Plan Schritt für Schritt.

    English meaning

    Then we will check the packages and prescribed plan step by step.

    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Mistake: Identify the insulin from pen colour alone.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Read the labels and compare them with the verified prescription. Ask Selin what she actually used without suggesting names. Mark unresolved details and contact the prescribing team. Do not infer dose or timing from colour.

    Why this helps¶

    A colour is not a reliable medicine identity. Agreement with a suggested name does not show that the patient remembers it.

    A small decision

    Your next step

    Selin identifies insulin by pen colour and agrees with suggested names. How should you verify the history?

    Read the explanation

    Best next move: Verify labels and actual use without leading prompts

    Verify labels and actual use independently. Do not choose the name Selin agrees with most quickly or assume that similarly coloured pens are interchangeable.

    A language-practice handover

    Say it to a colleague

    Lesen Sie Etiketten und vergleichen Sie sie mit der geprüften Verordnung. Fragen Sie Selin ohne Namensvorschläge nach der tatsächlichen Anwendung. Kennzeichnen Sie offene Details und fragen Sie das verordnende Team. Farbe bestimmt weder Dosis noch Zeitpunkt.

    Read the labels and compare them with the verified prescription. Ask Selin what she actually used without suggesting names. Mark unresolved details and contact the prescribing team. Do not infer dose or timing from colour.
