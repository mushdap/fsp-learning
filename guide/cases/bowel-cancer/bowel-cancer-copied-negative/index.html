    The symptom nobody actually asked about

    Yasmin reads her draft summary and notices “no weight change.” Nobody asked her about weight. She has been buying looser clothes but has not used a scale, and she assumed the clinician would ask if it mattered. The phrase came from a template, not from her account. A tempting response is to dismiss it as standard wording. The better task is to correct the record and ask a neutral question, without replacing one invented fact with a new measured weight loss that also has no evidence.

    Meet your patient
    Yasmin Stein, 53

    German words · human meaning

    Pull up a chair. Listen first.

    Yasmin reads her draft summary and notices “no weight change.” Nobody asked her about weight. Dr Daniyal appears as a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Da steht, mein Gewicht sei gleich. Das haben wir gar nicht besprochen.

    English meaning

    It says my weight is unchanged. We did not discuss that.

    Dr Daniyal

    Sie haben recht. Diese Angabe ist nicht belegt.

    English meaning

    You are right. That statement is not supported.

    Patient

    Meine Kleidung sitzt anders, aber gewogen habe ich mich nicht.

    English meaning

    My clothes fit differently, but I have not weighed myself.

    Dr Daniyal

    Ich korrigiere den Text und frage nach Ihrer Beobachtung und dem Zeitraum.

    English meaning

    I will correct the text and ask about your observation and the timeframe.

    Practice misstep

    The conversation gets stuck

    Practice misstep: Keep the phrase because it is standard wording.

    Better approach

    Make room for understanding

    Acknowledge that the negative statement was unsupported and correct it through the appropriate record process. Ask neutrally about weight and clothing changes, including timing and any reliable measurements. Document the answer as reported. Check the rest of the summary for template assumptions rather than expecting Yasmin to discover every inaccurate line herself.

    What changed—and why it helps¶

    A default sentence can look like a completed question once it enters a record. That creates false confidence for the next clinician. A correction should preserve the distinction between not asked, denied and measured. The patient’s observation may be relevant without becoming an exact number simply because the note needs filling.

    Your turn

    Yasmin finds an unasked negative in her draft summary. How should you repair it?

    Read the explanation

    Best next move: Correct the unsupported negative and ask neutrally

    The right move repairs both the wording and the information gap. Standard templates do not authorise invented negatives. An exact weight loss would be equally unsupported without a source. Record the reported change and uncertainty, then review whether other unchecked template statements have entered the draft.

    A language-practice handover

    Same person. A colleague needs the key details.
