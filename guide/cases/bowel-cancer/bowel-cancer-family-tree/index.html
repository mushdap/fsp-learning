    Which relative had which condition?

    David reports cancer in his family. His uncle had an unclear stomach condition, and his father had a polyp removed. The details are uncertain. David now has a bowel change that needs assessment.

    David berichtet über Krebs in der Familie. Der Onkel hatte etwas Unklares am Magen. Beim Vater wurde ein Polyp entfernt. Die Angaben sind unsicher. Davids neue Darmbeschwerden müssen beurteilt werden.

    See the differenceDen Unterschied erkennen

    1. 1Uncle: unclear stomach conditionOnkel: unklare Magenerkrankung
    2. 2Father: polyp removedVater: Polyp entfernt
    3. 3Inherited condition: not establishedErbliche Krankheit: nicht nachgewiesen

    Separate the relative, the diagnosis and the certainty.

    Angehörigen, Diagnose und Sicherheit der Angabe trennen.

    German words · human meaning

    Try these words

    Listen to the patient. Ask one clear question at a time. Dr Palwasha is a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Bei uns haben alle Darmkrebs, glaube ich.

    English meaning

    Everyone in our family has bowel cancer, I think.

    Dr Palwasha

    Welche Verwandten meinen Sie, und welche Diagnose wurde jeweils genannt?

    English meaning

    Which relatives do you mean, and what diagnosis was mentioned for each?

    Patient

    Beim Vater war es vielleicht nur ein Polyp.

    English meaning

    For my father it may only have been a polyp.

    Dr Palwasha

    Dann notieren wir das getrennt und markieren, was noch unklar ist.

    English meaning

    Then we will record that separately and mark what remains unclear.

    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Mistake: Record hereditary cancer as confirmed.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Ask which relative, which condition and the age at diagnosis. Seek records where possible. Separate a polyp from cancer and confirmed information from uncertain memory. Do not name an inherited syndrome from this account.

    Why this helps¶

    Family history needs specific details. Similar words and different procedures should not become one confirmed inherited diagnosis.

    Your next step

    David’s family cancer history contains uncertain diagnoses. How should you clarify it?

    Read the explanation

    Best next move: Separate relatives and verified versus uncertain diagnoses

    Keep useful family information while marking uncertainty. Missing details are a reason to clarify, not to confirm hereditary cancer or ignore the entire history.

    A language-practice handover

    Say it to a colleague

    Fragen Sie nach Angehörigem, Krankheit und Erkrankungsalter. Suchen Sie möglichst Unterlagen. Unterscheiden Sie Polypen von Krebs und sichere Angaben von Erinnerung. Stellen Sie daraus kein erbliches Syndrom fest.

    Ask which relative, which condition and the age at diagnosis. Seek records where possible. Separate a polyp from cancer and confirmed information from uncertain memory. Do not name an inherited syndrome from this account.

    Professional German example
    Herr Arendt berichtet über mögliche gastrointestinale Tumorerkrankungen in der Familie; beim Vater ist möglicherweise nur eine Polypektomie erfolgt. Betroffene Angehörige, Diagnosen und Erkrankungsalter werden getrennt erhoben, ein erbliches Syndrom ist nicht belegt.
