    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Mistake: Tell her to double the next dose whenever unsure.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Ask her to describe a recent day. Verify the medicine and prescribed schedule. Discuss barriers and acceptable reminders. Obtain individual advice from her treating team. Do not suggest doubling, stopping or changing doses.

    Why this helps¶

    A prescription does not show actual use. A specific day often gives clearer information than asking whether someone always follows a plan.
