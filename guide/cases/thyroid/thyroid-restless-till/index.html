    Shaking and a racing heartbeat

    Nadine reports shaking hands, unusual warmth and a racing heartbeat. Others blame her busy job. The duration and current severity are still unknown. She needs assessment of the symptoms, not a conclusion based on her work.

    Nadine berichtet über zitternde Hände, Wärmegefühl und Herzrasen. Andere geben der Arbeit die Schuld. Dauer und aktuelle Stärke sind noch unklar. Ihre Beschwerden brauchen eine Beurteilung, keine Vermutung aufgrund ihres Berufs.

    German words · human meaning

    Try these words

    Listen to the patient. Ask one clear question at a time. Dr Daniyal is a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Alle sagen, das sei nur Nervosität.

    English meaning

    Everyone says it is only nervousness.

    Dr Daniyal

    Herzklopfen und Zittern sollten wir genauer beurteilen.

    English meaning

    We should assess the palpitations and shaking more closely.

    Patient

    Also bilde ich mir das nicht einfach ein?

    English meaning

    So I am not simply imagining it?

    Dr Daniyal

    Ihre Beschwerden sind ernst zu nehmen; die Ursache ist noch offen.

    English meaning

    Your symptoms deserve attention; the cause is still open.

    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Mistake: Blame all symptoms on her busy job.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Ask whether symptoms are happening now, when they began and what accompanies them. Arrange prompt review when needed. Record her heartbeat sensation as reported until measurements are available. Keep possible causes open.

    Why this helps¶

    A stressful job may be relevant, but it does not establish the cause. A felt fast heartbeat is not a measured pulse.

    A small decision

    Your next step

    Nadine’s shaking and palpitations have been called only anxiety. What should the next assessment establish?

    Read the explanation

    Best next move: Assess the current symptoms and keep causes open

    Assess Nadine's current condition and take the symptoms seriously. Do not diagnose stress or thyroid disease from her job, and do not invent a pulse measurement.

    A language-practice handover

    Say it to a colleague

    Fragen Sie nach aktuellen Beschwerden, Beginn und Begleitzeichen. Veranlassen Sie bei Bedarf rasche Hilfe. Notieren Sie Herzrasen als Angabe, solange Messwerte fehlen. Lassen Sie mögliche Ursachen offen.

    Ask whether symptoms are happening now, when they began and what accompanies them. Arrange prompt review when needed. Record her heartbeat sensation as reported until measurements are available. Keep possible causes open.

    Professional German example
    Frau Kühn berichtet über Palpitationen, Händezittern und Wärmeempfindlichkeit. Aktuelle Ausprägung, Begleitsymptome und gemessene Herzfrequenz sind noch unbekannt; eine ausschließliche Stressursache oder Hyperthyreose ist nicht belegt.
