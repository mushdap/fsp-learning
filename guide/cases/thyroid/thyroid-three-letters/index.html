    What does TSH mean?

    Bastian is tired and brings thyroid blood results. He thinks a higher TSH always means the thyroid works harder. The results have not been interpreted in this scene. He needs a simple explanation of the signal.

    German words · human meaning

    Try these words

    Listen to the patient. Ask one clear question at a time. Dr Palwasha is a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Ist TSH das Schilddrüsenhormon selbst?

    English meaning

    Is TSH the thyroid hormone itself?

    Dr Palwasha

    TSH ist ein Steuersignal an die Schilddrüse.

    English meaning

    TSH is a control signal to the thyroid.

    Patient

    Warum schauen Sie dann noch andere Werte an?

    English meaning

    Why do you also look at other values, then?

    Dr Palwasha

    Weil Signal und Hormonwerte zusammen mit Ihren Beschwerden beurteilt werden.

    English meaning

    Because the signal and hormone values are assessed together with your symptoms.

    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Mistake: Diagnose from TSH alone without context.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Explain that TSH helps control the thyroid. It is different from thyroid hormones. The clinician reads these values together with symptoms and medicines. Arrange individual interpretation rather than diagnosing from TSH alone.

    Why this helps¶

    A control signal and the gland's hormone output are different pieces of information. One number does not explain the whole situation.

    Erklären Sie: TSH hilft, die Schilddrüse zu steuern. Es ist etwas anderes als Schilddrüsenhormone. Das Team betrachtet Werte, Beschwerden und Medikamente gemeinsam. Planen Sie eine persönliche Befundbesprechung statt einer Diagnose nur aus TSH.

    Explain that TSH helps control the thyroid. It is different from thyroid hormones. The clinician reads these values together with symptoms and medicines. Arrange individual interpretation rather than diagnosing from TSH alone.
