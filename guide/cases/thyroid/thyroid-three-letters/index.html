    Three letters and a very large worry

    Bastian brings a laboratory printout covered with question marks around TSH. He thought the larger number meant his thyroid must be working harder, then found an explanation saying the opposite can happen. He has been tired and wants a sentence he can remember without learning endocrinology overnight. No individual interpretation is supplied in this scene. Your task is to explain that TSH is a signal involved in controlling the thyroid and that the clinician interprets it with thyroid hormone results and the clinical context.

    Meet your patient
    Bastian Reim, 52
    Practise
    Explain TSH versus thyroid hormones simply
    Scroll to begin ↓
    German words · human meaning

    Pull up a chair. Listen first.

    Bastian brings a laboratory printout covered with question marks around TSH. Dr Palwasha appears as a fictional teaching character.

    Patient

    Ist TSH das Schilddrüsenhormon selbst?

    English meaning

    Is TSH the thyroid hormone itself?

    Dr Palwasha

    TSH ist ein Steuersignal an die Schilddrüse.

    English meaning

    TSH is a control signal to the thyroid.

    Patient

    Warum schauen Sie dann noch andere Werte an?

    English meaning

    Why do you also look at other values, then?

    Dr Palwasha

    Weil Signal und Hormonwerte zusammen mit Ihren Beschwerden beurteilt werden.

    English meaning

    Because the signal and hormone values are assessed together with your symptoms.

    Practice misstep

    The conversation gets stuck

    Practice misstep: Diagnose from TSH alone without context.

    Better approach

    Make room for understanding

    Use a brief signal-and-response explanation without turning it into a rule that every high or low value has one meaning. Ask Bastian to tell you the distinction back. Leave interpretation of his actual pattern to the responsible clinician, including relevant medicines and history. Explain what result discussion is arranged rather than guessing from one circled number.

    What changed—and why it helps¶

    A small conceptual distinction can remove a large misunderstanding. Bastian does not need a lecture on every exception to understand why more than one result matters. At the same time, an oversimplified rule can produce another false conclusion. The useful endpoint is a clear question for the clinician, not self-diagnosis from a printout.

    A small decision

    Your turn

    Bastian mistakes TSH for the thyroid hormone itself. What explanation is most useful?

    Read the explanation

    Best next move: Explain the signal distinction and arrange individual interpretation

    The correct move teaches enough to make the result discussion understandable. One value should not be treated as the whole clinical picture. A list of abbreviations may increase confusion. Use plain language, check the distinction and make clear which parts of Bastian’s individual result still need professional interpretation.

    A language-practice handover

    Same person. A colleague needs the key details.

    Herr Reim fragt nach der Bedeutung von TSH bei Müdigkeit und vorliegenden Laborunterlagen. Die individuelle Befundkonstellation ist in diesem Gespräch nicht interpretiert; Steuersignal und Schilddrüsenhormone werden sprachlich unterschieden.
