    English meaning

    What to avoid

    What to avoid

    Mistake: Say both names mean the same operation.

    A clearer answer

    A clearer answer

    Use a labelled drawing. The gallbladder stores bile; the bile duct carries it. Gallbladder removal and ERCP have different aims. Confirm which procedure the specialist actually recommends.

    Why this helps¶

    Two unfamiliar names can sound interchangeable. The body structure helps explain why the procedures have different purposes.
